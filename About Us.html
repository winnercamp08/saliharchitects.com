About Us

Salih Architects is a London-based design studio founded by ARB Chartered Architect Mahan Hafezi, specialising in timeless, elegant, and luxurious architecture and interiors.

At the heart of the studio is a belief that great design should feel both rooted and refined—grounded in classical proportions yet elevated by modern sensibilities. With a distinctive aesthetic influenced by French and neoclassical traditions, Mahan Hafezi leads the creative vision of every project, shaping spaces that are sophisticated, expressive, and deeply personal.

We work internationally, collaborating with private clients and developers to design high-end residences and bespoke architectural concepts. Our portfolio includes luxury villas, signature homes, and large-scale residential developments, always with an emphasis on refined detailing, spatial harmony, and lasting impact.

While Mahan Hafezi serves as the creative lead, Salih Architects is supported by a dedicated team of designers who share a commitment to beauty, precision, and innovation. Together, we bring each project to life—blending architectural clarity with rich interior narratives.

As a studio, we embrace the creative potential of technology. By integrating AI into parts of our design process, we explore new ways of visualising and refining ideas—while always staying true to our belief that human creativity, intuition, and emotion must remain at the core of design. Our approach is not about automation, but amplification: using technology to push the boundaries of what timeless architecture can be.

Salih Architects is a studio for those who seek spaces that are not only visually striking, but emotionally resonant—crafted with care, purpose, and enduring elegance.
